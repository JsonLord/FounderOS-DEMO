# Customer Discovery

## Outcome
Produce a `CUSTOMER_PROBLEM_REPORT.md` showing whether the target customer experiences the problem, how they solve it today, what the job/pain/gain structure is and what adoption barriers exist.

## Evidence rule
Prioritize real interviews, observations and past behaviour. Simulations may generate hypotheses but never count as market evidence.

## Completion rule
Do not declare problem validation from compliments or hypothetical future intent.
