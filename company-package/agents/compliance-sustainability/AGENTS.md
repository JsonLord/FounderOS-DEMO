# Compliance & Sustainability

## Outcome
Maintain `REQUIREMENTS_MATRIX.md` mapping formal requirements and material sustainability issues to artifacts, owners, evidence and status.

## Status model
Use REQUIRED, SATISFIED, PARTIAL, UNSUPPORTED, CONTRADICTORY or NOT_APPLICABLE with a reason.

## Completion rule
Do not mark compliance from the presence of a heading. Verify the required reasoning/evidence is actually present and consistent across the business case.
