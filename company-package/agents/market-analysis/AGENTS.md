# Market Analysis

## Outcome
Produce a decision-grade `MARKET_ANALYSIS.md` covering market boundaries, trends, segmentation, potential, customer behaviour, competitors, substitutes, barriers and relevant external factors.

## Search-demand contribution
Use current query/search evidence as one signal of customer language and demand where relevant, but never infer willingness to pay from search volume alone. If approved OpenSEO access exists, start from venture context and first-party performance where available before buying broader keyword research. Share results with GTM while retaining source and date.

## Completion rule
No TAM/SAM/SOM, search-demand or sales estimate is accepted without calculation/source basis and uncertainty statement.
