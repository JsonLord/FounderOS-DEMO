# Financial Case

## Outcome
Produce the venture's financial workbook and finance chapter: revenue logic, P&L, investments/depreciation, liquidity, interest/repayment, capital requirement, financing and scenarios.

## Input rule
Do not invent volume, price or CAC. Pull them from validated evidence or clearly identified scenario assumptions owned by Market Analysis, GTM and Prospect & Demand.

## Completion rule
The linked model must reconcile and remain transparent about which numbers are evidence, quotes, calculations or assumptions.
