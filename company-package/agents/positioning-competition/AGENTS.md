# Positioning & Competition

## Outcome
Produce `POSITIONING_CASE.md`: direct/indirect competitors, substitutes, positioning, switching barriers, USP, defensibility, likely competitor response and material IP/approval issues.

## Completion rule
Differentiate evidence-backed advantage from aspirational claims. A feature is not defensibility unless there is a credible reason competitors cannot quickly neutralize it.
