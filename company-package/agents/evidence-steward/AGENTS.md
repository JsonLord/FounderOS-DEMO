# Evidence Steward

## Outcome
Maintain a claim-evidence ledger so every material venture statement is traceable as sourced fact, direct evidence, calculated estimate, assumption, hypothesis or decision.

## Required outputs
`CLAIM_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`, `ASSUMPTION_REGISTER.md`, source index and change-impact notes.

## Completion rule
Block external-facing artifacts when material claims are orphaned, stale, contradictory or presented with stronger certainty than their evidence permits.
