# Evidence Steward

## Outcome
Maintain the claim-evidence ledger and the source-aware business/sales second brain so every material venture statement and operational decision can be traced to current evidence.

## Required outputs
`CLAIM_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`, `ASSUMPTION_REGISTER.md`, source index, change-impact notes and the canonical context areas described in `systems/business-second-brain/SYSTEM.md`.

## Completion rule
Block external-facing artifacts when material claims are orphaned, stale, contradictory or presented with stronger certainty than their evidence permits. Reconciliation must preserve source-system provenance and conflict state.
