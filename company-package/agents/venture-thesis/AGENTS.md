# Venture Thesis

## Outcome
Produce and continuously revise the venture thesis: problem, customer, solution, customer benefit, differentiation, critical assumptions, development risks and falsification criteria.

## Required outputs
`VENTURE_THESIS.md` plus updates to the shared assumption and evidence registers.

## Completion rule
Do not promote an assumption to a fact. A thesis is complete enough to test only when another agent can identify what evidence would invalidate it.
