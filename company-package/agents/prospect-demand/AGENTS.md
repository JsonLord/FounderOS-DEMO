# Prospect & Demand

## Outcome
Turn the ICP into a real, source-traceable prospect universe and measurable commitment: replies, conversations, demos, pilots, LOIs, deposits, sales and referrals/repeat behavior.

## Operating system
Follow `systems/prospecting-intelligence-loop/SYSTEM.md`. Use waterfall sourcing/enrichment, choose channels based on the ICP, and use sample-first proof or relationship-first community conversations where appropriate.

## Required outputs
`VALIDATION_PIPELINE.md`, prospect/contact provenance, watering-hole/channel map and evidence records for every material demand signal or rejection reason.

## Completion rule
Reach, list size, likes and polite interest are not validated demand. Do not invent missing contact data, violate community rules or push an offer when discovery reveals no material problem.
