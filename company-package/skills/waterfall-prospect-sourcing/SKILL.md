# Waterfall prospect sourcing

## Outcome
A deduplicated, normalized prospect universe with coverage and provenance rather than one vendor's incomplete list.

## Method
1. Translate ICP into machine-checkable inclusion/exclusion criteria.
2. Choose the highest-fidelity source for that ICP (for example professional/company databases vs local/maps-style sources).
3. Normalize and deduplicate before another source is queried.
4. Use broader web/AI discovery only to fill unresolved coverage gaps.
5. Record source and confidence per material field.
6. Audit a sample and report coverage limitations.

## Rule
Do not optimize for raw list size. Unknown or unverifiable companies remain unresolved, not invented.
