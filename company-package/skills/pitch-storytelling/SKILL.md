# Pitch storytelling

## Outcome
A standard pitch that communicates the validated business case clearly rather than inventing a parallel narrative.

## Story discipline
Find the three words that drive the story. Use ethos for credibility, pathos for emotion and logos for reason. Prefer concrete language when abstraction makes the value harder to understand.

## Standard pitch sequence
1. Company mission.
2. Problem and target audience.
3. Solution, value proposition, benefits and use cases.
4. Competition, market position and USPs.
5. Market size / TAM-SAM-SOM.
6. Market validation: demand, traction, customers/pipeline, LOIs.
7. Business model, revenue streams and value chain.
8. Go-to-market strategy and target audience.
9. Team skills, experience, strengths, weaknesses and why us.
10. Status, achievements, 1-3 year roadmap and mitigations.
Include defensibility: relevant technology, research and IP strategy.

Every material claim must trace back to venture evidence or be labelled an assumption.
