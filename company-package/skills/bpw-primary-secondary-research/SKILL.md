# BPW primary and secondary research

## Outcome
A research package that combines market evidence collected directly with credible published information.

## Direct research methods
Use customer interviews/surveys, focus groups, observation, competitor analysis, workshops, trade-fair/customer conversations and other fit-for-purpose tests. Define who is being researched, the questions, the method and the intended decision before collecting data.

## Indirect / secondary research
Use normally available sources such as statistical offices, chambers, industry associations, international organisations, banks/insurers, institutes, trade publications, company materials and relevant databases.

## Discipline
- Do not claim that market data do not exist until the usual sources have been checked.
- External expensive market studies are not automatically necessary for an SME-style business plan.
- Separate direct observations from published facts and from founder estimates.
- Prefer trends and defensible orders of magnitude over false precision.

## Completion gate
Each major market claim has a source type, source reference, date/context and confidence level.
