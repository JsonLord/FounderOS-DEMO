# BPW financial planning

## Outcome
A transparent, internally linked financial case showing profitability, liquidity, capital requirements and financing under realistic assumptions.

## Planning sequence
1. Build the profit-and-loss plan from revenue and expense assumptions.
2. Build investment and depreciation planning; investments consume cash while depreciation affects P&L.
3. Build liquidity planning using actual cash timing, not invoice timing, and derive the funding requirement from cumulative shortfalls.
4. Build interest and repayment planning; loan drawdowns and principal repayments affect liquidity, while interest affects both P&L and liquidity.
5. Recheck feedback loops until financing is sufficient and liquidity remains positive in all periods.
6. Run scenarios for material opportunities and risks.

## Horizon
Use a three-year view; the first year should normally be monthly, with later periods at a sensible quarterly or half-year granularity.

## Assumption discipline
Revenue must inherit validated/estimated price, customer volume and timing from market/GTM evidence. Explain every material revenue, cost, investment and funding assumption.

## Completion gate
The model exposes break-even, profit/loss by period, liquidity, funding need, funding sources and major sensitivities without unexplained inconsistencies.
