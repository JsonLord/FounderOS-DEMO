# Expert knowledge distillation

## Outcome
A reusable guide or skill whose claims remain traceable to source material and whose applicability limits are explicit.

## Method
Source/example → identify why it works → extract principles, decisions and checks → distinguish demonstrated facts from opinion → convert into an executable checklist/skill → apply to a real task → review result → update the skill.

## Rules
Do not treat popularity as proof. Preserve source attribution. Do not silently generalize creator-specific results into universal rules. A distilled skill should say what evidence would cause it to change.
