# BPW executive summary

## Outcome
A short, self-contained summary that motivates a reader to continue while accurately representing the full plan.

## Required content
Cover the product/service, USP, customer benefit, target groups, market potential, competitive situation, founding-team competence and responsibilities, capital requirement, expected profitability, and the venture's material sustainability contribution and objectives.

## Method
- Write the summary last, even though it appears first.
- Only summarize claims and numbers developed elsewhere in the plan.
- Recheck it whenever a core assumption or chapter changes.
- Use plain language that a non-specialist can understand in roughly five to ten minutes.
- Prefer precise evidence-backed statements over promotional language.

## Completion gate
Every material sentence maps to a current business-plan section and evidence or an explicitly labelled estimate.
