# BPW market analysis

## Outcome
A sourced market analysis that supports a go/no-go decision and provides defensible inputs for positioning, sales volumes and financial planning.

## Workflow
1. Define the research problem and decision objective.
2. Bound the relevant overall market and identify substitutes.
3. Identify important relationships and external influences.
4. Choose research methods.
5. Collect data.
6. Analyse and prepare the data.
7. Produce a decision recommendation.

## Required analysis
Describe market history/current state, size, growth, trends, political/legal/technological and other external factors, success factors, sustainability influences, target segments, market potential, buying motives/behaviour, direct and indirect competitors, substitute solutions, market-entry barriers and likely competitive response.

## Evidence rule
Reasoned estimates are allowed when exact data do not exist, but the basis, method, source and uncertainty must be explicit and plausible.

## Completion gate
The analysis can explain who may buy, how many may buy, why they may buy, what they use instead, and how the evidence supports realistic sales assumptions.
