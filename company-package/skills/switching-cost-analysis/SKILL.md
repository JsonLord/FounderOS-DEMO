# Switching cost analysis

## Outcome
A ranked switching-cost map plus actions that reduce the largest adoption barrier.

## Examine
- Time: learning and migration time.
- Money: direct financial cost of changing.
- Effort: physical, mental or emotional work required.
- Emotional cost: distress, uncertainty, loss of control or feeling unsafe.
- Risk: what could go wrong if the customer switches.

Identify the highest switching cost for the target customer and design an explicit mitigation for it. Feed the mitigation into the offer, onboarding, prototype and sales narrative.
