# BPW team competency case

## Outcome
An evidence-backed account of why the founding team can build, sell and operate the business.

## Required content
- Relevant technical/domain, commercial and personal competencies.
- Practical experience that matters to this venture rather than a generic CV chronology.
- Clear future roles and responsibilities for each founder.
- Competency-gap analysis and when/how each gap will be closed.
- Key external people, dependencies on them and mitigations if they leave.
- A deliberate, cost-aware staffing plan; do not fill every role prematurely.
- Reasonable founder compensation / entrepreneur salary assumptions.
- Clear ownership of sustainability responsibilities where applicable.

## Completion gate
Every mission-critical activity has an accountable capable owner now, a credible gap-closing plan, or an explicit unresolved risk.
