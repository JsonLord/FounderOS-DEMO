# Customer journey storyboarding

## Outcome
An end-to-end customer journey whose problem, friction and desired outcome are understandable without explanation.

## Method
1. Map what happens before, during and after the customer tries to complete the job.
2. Make the journey visible as a sequence of steps.
3. For important steps, list pains and gains.
4. Ask what the customer has already tried to remove those pains.
5. Test whether the proposed solution fits naturally into the routine.
6. Ask explicitly what the customer does not like about the solution to counter positivity bias.
7. Check whether the journey is easy to understand and whether the problem is actually mitigated.

## Output
Store journey steps, pains, gains, current workarounds, open assumptions and evidence references.
