# Customer discovery methods

## Outcome
Evidence about the customer's real life, problem and current behaviour.

## Methods
Use an appropriate mix of: talk to customers, observe them, walk in their shoes, and desktop research.

## Four rules
- Talk about their life, not your idea.
- Ask about specific events in the past, not generic opinions about the future.
- Talk less and listen more.
- Ask curious questions; questions are how uncertainty is reduced.

## Research design
Before running discovery define who to talk to, when/where/how, the ideal research output, and the discovery methods to use. Update the insight strategy after each batch.

## Simulation use
Simulated interviews may help generate hypotheses and probe pains/gains, but must be labelled simulation and must not be counted as market evidence.
