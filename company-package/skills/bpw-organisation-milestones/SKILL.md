# BPW organisation and milestones

## Outcome
An organisation and implementation plan that makes it clear how the venture creates value and who does what, when and with which resources.

## Required content
- Core value-creating activities and which remain internal vs outsourced/partnered.
- Functions, responsibilities and staffing of key functions.
- Business/legal form, management and ownership structure.
- Relevant location factors and a reasoned location choice.
- Medium- and long-term milestones that are optimistic but realistic.
- Dependencies between price/sales, production/procurement, personnel and investment planning.
- Personnel growth with role requirements and cost implications.
- Sustainability considerations in operations, supply chain, workplace and culture.

## Planning rule
A sales-volume milestone must propagate into capacity, staffing, investment and financial assumptions rather than exist as an isolated roadmap item.

## Completion gate
Every major milestone has prerequisites, owner, timing, measurable result and downstream resource/cost consequences.
