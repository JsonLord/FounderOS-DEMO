# Avatar → offer → landing page

## Outcome
A landing/fake-door proposition that is explicitly derived from the current customer evidence and offer rather than generic product features.

## Chain
1. Customer profile: segment, trigger, jobs, pains, gains, current alternatives, language and buying constraints.
2. Offer: promised outcome, scope, proof, price hypothesis, risk reversal/commitment and disqualifiers.
3. Page: headline, problem, desired outcome, mechanism, proof, offer terms, objections and CTA.

## Checks
Every important page claim maps to the offer or evidence. If ICP or offer changes, flag dependent page copy as stale. Generated drafts require review; model output is not customer evidence.
