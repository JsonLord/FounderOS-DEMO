# BPW market segmentation

## Outcome
A set of prioritized customer segments whose size, behaviour and reachability can be estimated.

## Candidate criteria
For consumer markets consider geographic, demographic, psychographic/lifestyle, buying-behaviour and usage characteristics. For business markets consider company size, industry, location, technology/standards, procurement and decision structures, order sizes, usage and service requirements.

## Segment tests
A useful segment should let the team estimate customer count and buying behaviour and should be reachable with a reasonably consistent sales approach. Prioritize segments where customer benefit and willingness to pay appear strongest, while documenting reasons not to focus on each alternative segment.

## Output
For each segment record definition, size estimate, needs/jobs, buying motives, information sources, purchase/usage frequency, current alternatives, expected response, sustainability expectations, reachability and evidence confidence.

## Completion gate
The chosen initial segment is narrower than the market, measurable enough for planning, and justified against alternatives.
