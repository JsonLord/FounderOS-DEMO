# Platform launch readiness

## Outcome
A launch-ready platform presence whose claims and content are grounded in tested venture evidence.

## Source-derived goal
The source explicitly names registering a Kickstarter account, filling it with content, and having a validated and tested prototype.

## Readiness gate
Before treating a platform launch as complete, require: a defined customer and problem, a concrete value proposition, a tested prototype, evidence of demand, a defensible competitor position, and content that matches those facts.

## Adaptation rule
The same readiness logic may be reused for another distribution platform, but platform-specific rules, permissions and formats must be researched separately; the source document itself does not define them.
