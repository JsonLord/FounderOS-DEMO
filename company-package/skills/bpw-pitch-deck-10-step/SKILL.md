# BPW ten-step pitch deck

## Outcome
A concise standard pitch whose slide claims are derived from the canonical business case rather than separately invented.

## Required sequence
1. Company mission: one simple declarative sentence.
2. Problem: problem, target audience and pain.
3. Solution: product/service/value proposition, benefits and use cases.
3B. Defensibility: relevant technology, research and IP strategy.
4. Competition: positioning/2x2 where useful, competitive advantage and USPs.
5. Market: market size and TAM/SAM/SOM when defensible.
6. Metrics & market validation: demand, sales, customers/pipeline, LOIs and other behavioural evidence.
7. Business model: revenue streams and value chain.
8. Go-to-market: strategy and target audience.
9. Team: skills, experience, strengths/weaknesses and why this team.
10. Status & future: achievements, 1-3 year roadmap, risk mitigations and the ask.

## Evidence rule
Every quantitative or traction claim must link to a source/evidence ID. Do not manufacture social proof, customers, market size or product capability for narrative quality.

## Completion gate
The deck tells one coherent story from problem to evidence to business model to ask and matches the current business plan/BMC/financial case.
