# BPW Business Model Canvas

## Outcome
A one-page nine-block business model plus explanatory notes that make the relationships between offer, customers, infrastructure and finances explicit.

## Reasoning order
Use the four BPW lenses: `What?` products/services and value proposition; `Who?` customer segments, relationships and channels; `How?` key activities, resources and partnerships; `How much?` revenue streams and cost structure.

## Required links
- Offer packages must map to specific customer segments and problems/needs.
- Relationships and channels must fit those segments and have cost implications.
- Activities, resources and partners must be necessary to deliver the value proposition, channels, relationships and revenue streams.
- Revenue streams must explain what customers pay for, willingness to pay, pricing mechanism and contribution to turnover.
- Cost structure must follow from the activities/resources/partners/relationships/channels selected.
- Sustainability opportunities and requirements must be integrated into the relevant blocks.

## Completion gate
No Canvas block contradicts another; every important element has evidence/confidence and unresolved assumptions are visible.
