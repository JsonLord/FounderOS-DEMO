# Design judgment stack

## Outcome
A fake-door or campaign surface that is credible, coherent and appropriate to the proposition without generic AI visual defaults.

## Method
Maintain a curated design reference/index with source, rationale, examples and anti-patterns. For a new page, select a small number of relevant principles based on audience, positioning, offer and use context; produce multiple directions when uncertainty is high; review against usability, hierarchy, trust, CTA clarity and brand/reference consistency.

## Rule
Design quality supports the experiment but is not demand evidence. Do not blindly combine every design heuristic in the library.
