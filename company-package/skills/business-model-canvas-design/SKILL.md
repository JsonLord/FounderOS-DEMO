# Business Model Canvas design

## Outcome
A coherent Business Model Canvas whose cells are supported by the venture's evidence.

## Components to resolve
- Key partnerships: partners, resources and activities received from them, including partners relevant to sustainability requirements.
- Key activities: required activities, what is internal vs partnered, and the implementation roadmap.
- Key resources: required resources, location factors, legal form, roles and competencies.
- Value propositions: packages by segment, needs/problems solved, exact customer benefit and sustainability contribution.
- Customer relationships: relationship type, expectations, cost and integration into the model.
- Channels: communication and sales channels, cost, process integration and which work best.
- Customer segments: most important customers, market structure, trends, competitors and relevant sustainability principles.
- Revenue streams: pre-revenue financing, willingness to pay, price strategy and contribution of each stream to total revenue.
- Cost structure: derive the costs required by the activities, resources, relationships and channels even though the source worksheet excerpt does not provide a separate cost-structure question block.

## Rule
Mark unsupported cells as assumptions; do not invent missing evidence.
