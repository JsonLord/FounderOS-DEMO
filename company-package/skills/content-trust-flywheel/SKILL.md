# Content trust flywheel

## Outcome
Content that makes the intended customer understand why an evidenced problem/benefit matters and creates measurable next actions.

## Method
Maintain a library of foundational themes, customer language and proven/outlier examples. Develop one strong core idea, then adapt it into channel-native long/short formats while preserving the underlying claim and offer. Feed high-performing angles back into the library.

## Quality test
Content must answer: who is this for, why should they care now, what specific pain/benefit is being made concrete, what evidence supports it, and what next action is appropriate?

## Rule
Do not auto-spam every channel merely because generation is cheap. Measure qualified downstream behavior.
