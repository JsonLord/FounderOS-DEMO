# BPW sustainability integration

## Outcome
A cross-functional sustainability case that improves or stress-tests the business model instead of adding a standalone ESG paragraph.

## Source framing
BPW treats sustainable development as a cross-cutting entrepreneurial task across economic, environmental and social dimensions (triple bottom line).

## Apply to the business model
Identify relevant opportunities and risks across R&D, procurement, production/service delivery, product quality/information, customer protection/inclusion, supplier fairness, climate/resource use, energy, local environmental effects, workplace practices, equality/diversity, safety, training, employee participation and community impact.

## Required reasoning
For each relevant field define: issue, materiality to this venture, target, measure, owner, evidence/metric, timing, cost and expected business effect such as savings, revenue, resilience, risk reduction or access to resources/customers.

Ensure sustainability implications are propagated into product policy, communication, channels, key activities/resources/partners, organisation and finance.

## Completion gate
Claims of sustainability have concrete business-model relevance and measurable actions; material negative impacts and trade-offs are not hidden.
