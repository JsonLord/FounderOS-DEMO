# Jobs to be done

## Outcome
A customer job model that distinguishes what the customer needs to accomplish, feel and signal socially.

## Categories
- Functional jobs: the practical task or goal to complete, such as saving time, organizing information, learning a skill or managing money.
- Emotional jobs: feelings associated with completing the job, such as safety, confidence, reduced stress, comfort or entertainment.
- Social jobs: how completing the job affects status or interaction, such as belonging to a community, showing responsibility or contributing to change.

## Use
Tie every proposed feature or service element to at least one evidenced job. Remove or question solution elements that cannot be connected to a customer job.
