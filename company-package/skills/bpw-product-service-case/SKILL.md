# BPW product/service case

## Outcome
A product/service description that makes the customer problem, benefit and competitive advantage obvious to a non-specialist.

## Required reasoning
- Define the offer clearly and avoid unnecessary technical detail.
- Describe the customer problem or need from the customer's perspective.
- State the concrete benefit; customers buy outcomes such as time, cost, quality, convenience, status, information advantage or other value, not technical specifications alone.
- Compare relevant existing alternatives and explain the USP.
- State development status, prototypes, pilot/test customers and remaining development steps.
- Identify development costs and risks, required approvals/licences, state of the art, IP/protection strategy and how advantage can be sustained.
- Integrate relevant sustainability effects across suppliers, production and delivery.

## Completion gate
The chain `customer need -> offer -> benefit -> target group -> evidence -> development risk` is explicit and consistent.
