# Venture gap analysis

## Outcome
Produce a gap register that makes the venture testable instead of merely plausible.

## Questions to resolve
1. Who are the customers?
2. What problem is being solved for them?
3. What is the solution or what must it contain?
4. Who are the competitors?
5. How will a prototype be tested?
6. What is the value proposition?
7. How will the product/service be brought to market?
8. What is the market?
9. Which founder skills, network and resources create an advantage?
10. What are the solution's unique selling points?

## Completion rule
Do not mark complete while a material question is unanswered. Record each gap as known, assumption, evidence-needed, or resolved, and assign the next evidence-producing action.
