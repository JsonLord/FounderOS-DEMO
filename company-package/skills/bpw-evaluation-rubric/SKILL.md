# BPW evaluation rubric

## Outcome
A structured reviewer report that identifies missing, weak, contradictory or unsupported parts before an external evaluator does.

## Plan checks
Evaluate understandability for non-specialists, product/USP/customer benefit, target-group definition, development status, team competence and commercial capability, market/industry/trends/segmentation/potential, competition and sources, price and plan-revenue logic, target-fit communications and distribution, market-entry measures, organisation/roles/personnel/milestones/legal/location, P&L/liquidity/funding gap/financing, sustainability and overall coherence.

## Canvas checks
Evaluate overview/readability/coherence, value proposition, segmentation/market/competition, relationships/channels, key activities/resources/partners, pricing/revenue/cost structure, sustainability, and a realistic three-to-five-year outlook with opportunities and risks.

## Review behaviour
- Score evidence quality separately from writing quality.
- Flag contradictions between artifacts even when each section is individually plausible.
- Require remediation actions for every material weakness.
- Do not improve a score by filling missing facts yourself.

## Completion gate
Return a decision with blockers, severity, owner and required evidence for each blocker.
