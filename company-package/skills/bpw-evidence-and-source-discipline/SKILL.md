# BPW evidence and source discipline

## Outcome
A claim-evidence ledger that lets every material venture statement be traced and challenged.

## Claim classes
Label each material claim as one of: sourced fact, direct customer/market observation, calculated estimate, founder assumption, hypothesis to test, or decision.

## Source expectations
Use normally obtainable market information and direct customer research before declaring evidence unavailable. Published sources can include public statistics, chambers/associations, banks/insurers, institutes, industry databases/publications and company materials. Primary evidence can include customers, prospective customers, competitors and relevant events/trade fairs.

## Estimate rules
When exact figures are unavailable, estimates may be used if their basis is secure enough, method is explicit, logic is understandable, assumptions are visible and uncertainty is not disguised as precision.

## Propagation rule
When a source or assumption changes, identify all dependent artifacts: thesis, market analysis, BMC, GTM, financial plan, business plan and pitch.

## Completion gate
No material external-facing claim is orphaned from evidence or an explicit assumption label.
