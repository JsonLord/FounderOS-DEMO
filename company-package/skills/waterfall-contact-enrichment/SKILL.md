# Waterfall contact enrichment

## Outcome
For each qualified organization, identify the most relevant decision maker/influencer and a permitted, verifiable contact path.

## Method
Start with structured high-confidence sources; inspect company-owned/public sources for missing roles; use web/AI discovery for gaps; verify identity/role independently when practical; then find/validate the contact channel. Preserve provenance.

## Rules
Do not generate email addresses and label them verified. Do not collect sensitive personal data unnecessary to the business purpose. Respect opt-outs and channel/platform restrictions.
