# Competitor landscape

## Outcome
A competitor landscape that supports positioning and exposes weak differentiation.

## Questions
- Who are the direct and indirect competitors today?
- Who could solve the same customer problem in a completely different way?
- How do competitors position themselves?
- How do we differentiate?
- What can we offer that competitors cannot easily copy?
- Which unique skills, network, knowledge or other assets do we possess?
- What is the unique selling point?

## Output
Maintain competitor profiles and a 2x2 positioning view when useful. Distinguish demonstrated differentiation from founder assumptions.
