# Diagnostic community outreach

## Outcome
A genuine peer conversation either reveals a material problem and advances to discovery, or establishes that no sales step is warranted.

## Stages
1. Contextual opener with no product pitch.
2. Peer questions about their business/workflow; one or two at a time.
3. Diagnosis: reflect an observed pattern or consequence and ask whether it is true.
4. Bridge: only after they acknowledge a meaningful problem, offer a short call focused on understanding that problem.

## Rules
Do not pitch in the first message on relational channels. Do not manufacture urgency. If no meaningful problem appears, stop selling and keep the relationship clean. Draft assistance must preserve the operator's real identity and voice.
