# Sample-first offer validation

## Outcome
A qualified prospect sees concrete proof relevant to their own situation and either advances, rejects, or supplies useful evidence.

## Method
1. Pick a narrow ICP and one painful job.
2. Define the smallest ethical sample that demonstrates the outcome without delivering the entire engagement.
3. Create 3–5 portfolio/reference examples if needed, clearly labelled as samples when they are not client work.
4. For high-fit prospects, personalize the sample to observable public or permissioned context.
5. Send the result with a low-friction invitation to discuss the underlying problem.
6. Record response, objection, call, commitment and referral/repeat signal.

## Rules
The sample is not evidence until a real prospect reacts. Respect IP, privacy and platform rules; never imply endorsement or fabricate a customer relationship.
