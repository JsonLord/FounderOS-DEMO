# Value proposition design

## Outcome
A concrete value proposition that can be understood and tested.

## Customer profile
Capture customer jobs, pains before/during/after the job, barriers and frustrations, and desired gains or outcomes.

## Value map
Describe products/services, pain relievers and gain creators. Explain how the solution removes barriers, improves underperforming alternatives, meets needs, outperforms the current solution and reduces adoption difficulty.

## Mission formula
`Our mission is to solve [problem] for [specific user] who struggle with [pain point], by providing [solution] that helps them [value/outcome].`

## Discipline
Define the problem before spending significant time and resources generating solutions. Revisit the proposition until it is concrete and understandable.
