# BPW business-plan governance

## Outcome
A business plan that is complete for the chosen BPW maturity stage, internally coherent, concise, understandable to non-specialists, and traceable to evidence.

## Source-derived structure
The complete BPW Plan structure contains: executive summary, product/service, founding team, market analysis, marketing, company/organisation, and financial planning/financing. Sustainability is a cross-cutting requirement and must be integrated into the relevant chapters rather than isolated as decoration.

## Governance rules
- Treat the plan as a living model: new evidence must update every dependent assumption, projection and chapter.
- Preserve causal consistency across customer benefit, target group, market size, pricing, sales volume, organisation and finance.
- Prefer essential, complete information over long detail; keep technical depth in appendices when it harms readability.
- Consolidate contributions through one final editor so tone, depth, definitions and numbers remain consistent.
- Weaknesses must be paired with mitigation or an explicit open action.
- Do not add facts to the executive summary that are not substantiated later in the plan.

## Completion gate
No chapter is complete if a material claim conflicts with another chapter, lacks an evidence/assumption label, or violates the applicable BPW requirements.
