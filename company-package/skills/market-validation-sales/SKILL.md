# Market validation through sales

## Outcome
Evidence of demand, not positive opinions.

## Evidence to gather
Track sales/traction, customers and pipeline, and letters of intent. Where appropriate use good offers and nudges rather than a pure push strategy, co-creation around sampling, product-sampling MVPs, and LOIs before production.

For B2B, account for negotiation, customization, relationship building, fewer but larger orders, higher volume with lower frequency, and potentially long sales processes.

## Reflection
Continuously answer: Who are we selling to? Do we understand their needs and pains? What value do they gain versus an alternative? Which sales activities create the strongest leads?

Do not equate reach, likes, simulated responses or compliments with validated demand.
