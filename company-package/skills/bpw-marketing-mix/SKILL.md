# BPW marketing mix

## Outcome
A target-segment-specific market-entry plan that connects customer benefit to product policy, pricing, communications, sales channels, costs and revenue assumptions.

## Product policy
Define the offer range, variants by customer segment, modifications/innovation and brand policy needed to satisfy the evidenced need.

## Pricing policy
Base price ranges on willingness to pay and customer value while checking cost economics. Explain positioning and whether the strategy resembles penetration, skimming or another mechanism. Include discounts/payment terms only when their effect on finance is modelled.

## Communication policy
Choose channels and measures that reach the actual decision makers: advertising, direct marketing, events/trade fairs, PR, customer visits, social/new media or other fitting channels. Estimate timing and costs.

## Distribution policy
Choose direct vs indirect routes according to product complexity, B2B/B2C structure, buying habits, customer count and price level. Explain what is internal vs partner-led and the time/cost of acquisition.

## Completion gate
Product, price, communication and distribution reinforce the same positioning and segment. Marketing costs feed the financial plan; plan revenue derives from plausible quantity x price.
