# Watering-hole discovery

## Outcome
A ranked channel/community map with an ethical entry plan.

## Rank by
ICP density, activity/recency, buyer relevance, openness of discussion, access requirements, promotion/research rules, cost, saturation and observable path to conversation.

Search not only obvious industry groups but also product/vendor ecosystems, trade communities, events and niche terminology used by the ICP.

## Entry plan
Read rules, observe norms, create/maintain an honest profile, contribute useful context before asking, and track whether the community actually yields learning or qualified conversations.

## Rule
Never lie to pass membership questions or treat private groups as scrape targets.
