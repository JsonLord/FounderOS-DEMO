# Transcript 01 — sample-first launch

## Source contribution
The source proposes a five-step launch pattern: form an initial market/pricing hypothesis, create 3–5 examples that demonstrate delivery, establish only the minimum credible presence needed for the channel, send a personalized free sample to a real prospect, then deliver quickly and ask for referrals.

## Founder OS adaptation
Adopt proof-before-infrastructure and sample-first offer tests. AI-generated market/pricing suggestions are hypotheses only; actual validation must come from real prospects. Prefer a concrete before/after or useful sample when the service can be demonstrated safely and cheaply.

## Loop
Hypothesis → sample → qualified prospect → personalized proof → conversation → paid/committed work → delivery → referral/repeat signal → evidence ledger.

## Guardrails
Do not misuse third-party IP, imply endorsement, fabricate client work, or publish altered customer assets without permission. Do not spend weeks on branding or infrastructure when a simpler artifact can test the demand assumption.
