# Transcript 07 — relationship-first community prospecting

## Source contribution
The source recommends finding concentrated 'watering holes' where the ICP already gathers, evaluating groups by relevance and activity, establishing a credible real profile/presence, participating before pitching, and moving conversations from peer questions to diagnosis. A call is offered only after the prospect acknowledges a meaningful problem; if no problem appears, do not force a sale.

It also proposes a watering-hole research skill and a DM co-pilot that classifies conversation stage and suggests the next human message.

## Founder OS adaptation
Generalize beyond Facebook: discover communities, product ecosystems, forums and groups where the ICP is naturally active. Rank them by ICP density, activity, access rules, promotion norms and observed conversion. Use relationship-first outreach on relational channels.

## Guardrails
Never lie to gain group access, impersonate an industry member, hide a commercial identity when disclosure is required, mass-pitch a community, or violate group/platform rules. Claims that one platform is 'best' remain hypotheses to test by ICP.
