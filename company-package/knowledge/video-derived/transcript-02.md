# Transcript 02 — one-person business with AI

## Source contribution
The source argues that AI is a catalyst for the same business fundamentals rather than a replacement for them: traffic, content, product/service, customer understanding, offers, landing pages, selling and iteration. It warns that autonomous agents without quality judgment can create activity without outcomes.

It gives a useful dependency chain: persistent customer avatar → offer blueprint → landing-page copy, with each artifact feeding the next and requiring review for hallucinations. It also proposes distilling strong expert examples into reusable guides/prompts and turning long-form ideas into a multi-format content ecosystem.

## Founder OS adaptation
Use AI to compress learning and execution, but require explicit quality criteria and iteration. Maintain reusable customer/offer context, not one-off prompts. Content exists to build trust and move an evidenced offer toward a qualified audience, not to spam every platform.
