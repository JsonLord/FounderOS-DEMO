# Prospecting intelligence loop

## Flow
ICP criteria → waterfall company sourcing → normalization/deduplication → waterfall contact enrichment → qualification → channel choice → outreach/community conversation → reply classification → call/commitment → campaign auto-research → ICP/message/channel update.

## Data discipline
Every prospect field records source, retrieval date and confidence when material. Cheap/high-fidelity sources run before broad/expensive discovery. Unknown stays unknown; do not synthesize a person or email to complete a row.

## Recurring analysis
On a defined cadence, compare response and conversion by segment attributes, trigger, source, channel, offer and message. Recommend one or a small number of testable changes, not simultaneous uncontrolled rewrites.

## Stop rules
Do not continue contacting disqualified prospects, people who opt out, communities where promotion is prohibited, or segments whose repeated evidence contradicts the thesis.
