# Content-to-demand loop

## Purpose
Make content a validation and trust engine rather than a publishing quota.

## Flow
Evidence/customer language → customer avatar/segment → offer → proof/sample → core message → channel-native content variants → distribution → CTA/fake door/conversation → call/commitment/payment → campaign analysis → update evidence, offer and content.

## Content flywheel
One strong source idea may become long-form explanation, newsletter/article, social posts, carousel, short video, FAQ, landing-page section and sales enablement, but each derivative must preserve the same evidenced proposition and platform context.

## Measurement
Track content by qualified downstream behavior. Reach/impressions are diagnostic; the decision metrics are qualified visits, replies, conversations, calls, trials, deposits, purchases and repeat/referral behavior as appropriate.

## Rule
Do not publish more formats merely because generation is cheap. Expand only content angles that earn attention from the intended segment or teach the team something material.
